❓ Frequently Asked Questions

                        

                            Q: Can I share a photo of my activity?
                            
A: Yes. When you Save Activity, you can optionally share a photo and caption to the Team Activity Feed so colleagues can cheer you on.

                        

                        

                            Q: How do I add my steps?
                            
A: Use the in-app Live KM / Step Counter only. Start Counting, walk/run, then Save. Manual entry and screenshots are not used.

                        

                        

                            Q: Do I need to provide a screenshot?
                            
A: No. All activity is tracked inside this app with the Live KM / Step Counter. Screenshots are not required.

                        

                        

                            Q: How is my daily goal calculated?
                            
A: This is a progressive 7-day challenge. Day 1 = 1 KM, Day 2 = 2 KM, ... up to Day 7 = 7 KM. For step tracking we use ~1,300 steps per KM (e.g. Day 3 goal ~ 3,900 steps). Complete your day's KM distance using the in-app Live KM / Step Counter, then Save.

                        

                        

                            Q: Who can join and how are winners chosen?
                            
A: Open to all CSG employees. There are 7 winners — one every day (26 July – 1 August). Ranking uses the shortest time to reach that day’s target KM on a single attempt (e.g. Day 1 = time to 1 KM, even if you ran 3 KM). Your best attempt updates the leaderboard.

                        

                        

                            Q: How do I install the Android app?
                            
A: First uninstall any older WOW-CSG Fitness app. Then open Android App, tap Download Android APK, allow install from unknown sources if asked, then Install. Sign in with your CSG email. Best for lock-screen step tracking.

                        

                        

                            Q: What if I forgot my password?
                            
A: On the Login tab, tap Forgot Password and enter your CSG email (@csgi.com). Check Inbox and Spam for the Firebase reset link. Or contact support at wow-csg@csgi.com.

                        

                        

                            Q: How do I check my ranking?
                            
A: Your current rank is displayed in the stats cards. You can also view the full leaderboard below your dashboard.
